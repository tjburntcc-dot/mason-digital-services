Privacy

Last updated 24 August 2026.

Hemmer Digital is operated by Mason Hemmer. This page describes what this website does and does not do with information.

What this site collects

This website is a static site. The public contact form opens your email app with a drafted message to masonhemmer@icloud.com.

If you email that address, the contents of your message, your email address, and any files you attach are stored in ordinary email, and are used to reply and to do the work you asked for.

Hosting and logs

The host that serves these files (for example GitHub Pages or another static host) may keep standard request logs such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested. Hemmer Digital does not run a separate analytics product on this site.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set advertising cookies and does not embed third-party trackers. If a future host requires a functional cookie, this page will be updated.

Sample builds

The sample builds use fictional businesses and synthetic data. Do not enter real customer, payment, or health information into a demo.
