Fixture tests

Five authored cases. Expected results live in fixtures/expected.json. This button runs the same engine the form uses.

Assumptions, limits, review

Assumptions. Service area is a ZIP allow-list. Urgency is keyword-based, not a trained model. CRM match is phone-only. A human still calls the customer.

What was automated. Validation, scoring, CRM match, routing, ticket JSON, and customer/owner copy. No SMS or calendar write.

What was manually reviewed. Each fixture’s expected route, emergency flag, and existing-customer match.

Limitations. Keyword scoring will miss unusual phrasing. This is not connected to a real HVAC board. Do not enter real customer data.
